    03 / Model matters

    Pick the right calculator guide

    Similar-looking Casio models may have different menus and features. Do not copy a button sequence from another model without checking.

    EX

    ClassWiz EX

    fx-991EX / fx-570EX. Icon-based apps, Equation/Func, Statistics, Vector, and Distribution functions depending on model and region.

    Official EX quick-start guide ↗
    CW

    New ClassWiz CW

    fx-991CW / fx-570CW. Home-screen apps and a different menu flow. Solver uses a numerical method and can return only one solution per run.

    Official CW user guide ↗
    ES+

    Classical ES Plus

    fx-991ES PLUS / fx-570ES PLUS. MODE-based menus. Some newer distribution and app shortcuts found on other models may not exist here.

    Official ES Plus user guide ↗
